CWR Seller Automation Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CWR Seller Automation accesses, uses, stores, and protects information obtained through eBay APIs and related seller operations.

Effective August 24, 2026

1. Scope and purpose

CWR Seller Automation is a private application operated by Turner Tech LLC for the operator's own eBay seller account. It is not currently offered as a public marketplace, consumer service, or multi-seller software platform.

The application is used to support inventory management, listing management, order processing, fulfillment, seller-account administration, analytics, and related operational workflows.

2. Information accessed or processed

Depending on the enabled eBay capabilities, CWR Seller Automation may access or process:

  • Seller and account information, including seller account identifiers, business policies, account settings, and API capability information.
  • Listing and inventory information, including SKUs, product details, images, prices, quantities, categories, item specifics, listing identifiers, and listing status.
  • Order and buyer information, including eBay-provided buyer identifiers, order identifiers, purchased items, quantities, shipping information, fulfillment status, and other order details needed to complete a transaction.
  • Fulfillment information, including shipping services, shipment status, carrier information, and tracking numbers.
  • Analytics and seller-performance information, including traffic, seller standards, and customer-service metrics made available by eBay.
  • Financial information made available through eBay APIs, such as transaction, fee, payout, or reconciliation data when the corresponding functionality is enabled.
  • Authentication information, including OAuth authorization and refresh tokens required to access eBay APIs. Application secrets and token values are treated as confidential credentials.

3. How information is used

Information obtained through eBay is used only as reasonably necessary to operate and maintain CWR Seller Automation, including to:

  • create, update, monitor, and reconcile eBay listings and inventory;
  • retrieve and process eBay orders;
  • support shipment and fulfillment workflows;
  • monitor seller performance and operational health;
  • reconcile marketplace, supplier, and financial records;
  • troubleshoot failures, maintain audit records, and protect the integrity and security of the integration; and
  • comply with eBay requirements and applicable legal obligations.
CWR Seller Automation does not sell eBay user information or use buyer information for unrelated advertising, profiling, or data-broker activities.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Information is not sold or rented. It may be transmitted to eBay, CWR Wholesale Distribution, hosting or infrastructure providers, and other service providers only when necessary to perform the seller workflow, maintain the application, secure the system, or satisfy legal requirements.

Service providers are expected to receive only the information reasonably necessary for the applicable operational purpose.

5. Storage and security

CWR Seller Automation uses technical and administrative safeguards intended to protect stored information and credentials. Sensitive configuration values and authorization credentials are not intentionally exposed through public application interfaces or logs.

Access to the application's administrative functions is restricted to authorized operators. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but reasonable controls are used to reduce unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

6. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for operational, reconciliation, security, audit, tax, accounting, or other legitimate legal purposes. Data that is no longer required is deleted or de-identified where appropriate.

When eBay provides a valid Marketplace Account Deletion or Closure notification for an eBay user, information associated with that user will be deleted as required by eBay unless specific information must be retained to satisfy a demonstrable legal obligation. Any legally required retention will be limited to the information and period necessary for that obligation.

7. OAuth access and revocation

Access to eBay APIs is authorized through eBay OAuth. The connected eBay account may revoke the application's authorization through eBay account settings. Revocation prevents future API access using the revoked authorization, subject to eBay's token and authorization behavior.

8. Public privacy-policy page

This page is provided publicly so eBay and other interested parties can review the privacy practices applicable to CWR Seller Automation. The policy page itself does not provide an account sign-up flow and is separate from the private seller application.

Standard hosting infrastructure may process ordinary web request information, such as IP address, browser information, timestamps, and requested URLs, for security, reliability, and server logging purposes.

9. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the application's capabilities, eBay requirements, or applicable legal obligations change. Material revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact
